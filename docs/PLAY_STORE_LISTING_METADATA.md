```text
Take control of your monthly salary before you spend it. 

SalaryLane is a 100% offline, private personal budget planner designed specifically for Indian salaried professionals and families. Allocate your salary, track daily living expenses, and see your exact financial standing on one executive infographic dashboard.

WHY SALARYLANE?

► SINGLE-SCREEN EXECUTIVE POSTER
Forget complex multi-tab apps that bury your budget across 10 submenus. SalaryLane gives you a clean, poster-style dashboard displaying your complete monthly reality: salary, essential living costs, savings, and remaining cashflow.

► TAILORED FOR INDIAN HOUSEHOLDS
Pre-built with categories that reflect real Indian financial lives:
• Rent, Groceries, Gas, Electricity & Wi-Fi
• House Help & Maid
• SIP (Mutual Funds) & Emergency Fund
• LIC & Insurance Policies
• Sending Money to Parents / Family Support
• Amortized Annual Expenses (Yearly Domestic & Hometown trips divided by 12)

► 100% OFFLINE & ABSOLUTELY PRIVATE
• Zero bank logins required
• Zero SMS reading or OTP scraping
• Zero account registration or cloud trackers
• All financial records reside strictly on your device in local SQLite.

► REAL BACKUP & COMPLETE RESTORE
Never lose your financial history when upgrading or changing phones. Export a complete, versioned JSON archive of all historical transactions, categories, and monthly plans to your Google Drive or local storage, and restore it on any new device in seconds.

► PUBLICATION-QUALITY VECTOR PDF & IMAGE EXPORTS
Generate high-resolution A4 PDF reports and PNG infographic statements with planned vs actual breakdown, donut charts, and spending variances. Ready to share with your spouse, family, or financial advisor in 1 tap.

► TRUTHFUL, ACCURATE ACCOUNTING
• Plan vs Actual toggle: compare what you intended to spend against what you actually spent.
• Zero-income honesty: if you haven't received your salary yet, the app accurately shows ₹0 without faking numbers.
• Integer minor-unit math (Paise precision): zero floating-point rounding errors.

KEY FEATURES AT A GLANCE:
• Quick transaction logging in under 3 seconds
• Dynamic category reordering and custom emoji categories
• Responsive single-column phone layout and tablet support
• Dark mode and adjustable text sizing
• High-performance, battery-friendly offline architecture

SalaryLane is an independent personal recordkeeping tool, not a bank, lender, or personalized investment advisor.
```

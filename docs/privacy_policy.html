Privacy Policy for SalaryLane: Budget Planner

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

Application: SalaryLane: Budget Planner (Package: com.salarylane.app)

Core Privacy Guarantee: SalaryLane operates 100% locally on your Android device. We do not require account registration, do not access your bank accounts or SMS messages, and never transmit your financial records to any external cloud servers.

1. Information We Handle

All information entered into SalaryLane is stored exclusively on your device within an encrypted local Room SQLite database. This information includes:

  • Monthly In-Hand Salary: Rupee amounts you manually specify for budgeting.
  • Category Allocations: Planned amounts across essential expenses, lifestyle items, and savings (e.g., Rent, Groceries, SIP, LIC, EMI).
  • Logged Transactions: Date, amount, category, account type (Cash/Bank), and optional note for each entry.
  • User Preferences: Display city name, dark/light theme, and text scaling preferences.

2. Data Collection and Transmission

We collect zero personal data off your device.

  • No network tracking or background telemetry SDKs are embedded in the application.
  • No SMS reading permissions are requested or used.
  • No bank account credentials, OTPs, or API integrations are requested.

3. Local Data Export and Backup

SalaryLane provides user-initiated export features:

  • PDF & PNG Reports: Generated locally on your device for personal archiving or sharing with family via the standard Android system share sheet.
  • CSV Spreadsheets: Exported directly to your local storage.
  • JSON Full Database Backups: Saved exclusively to the destination you choose (e.g., local storage, SD card, or your personal Google Drive). SalaryLane does not maintain any copy of your backups.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data resides on your device:

  • You can clear individual categories, months, or transactions directly within the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling SalaryLane permanently removes all locally stored SQLite databases and preferences.
  • To preserve your records before changing devices, use the built-in "Export Full Backup" feature.

5. Children's Privacy

SalaryLane is designed for household personal financial management and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

6. Changes to this Policy

If our privacy practices change, this policy will be updated with an adjusted Effective Date. Any material changes will be documented in the app's release notes on Google Play.
